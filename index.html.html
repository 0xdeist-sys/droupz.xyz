FAQ

No unnecessary steps.

Do I need to stake my DROUPZ?

No. Your DROUPZ remains in your wallet.

Do I need to claim rewards?

No claim transaction is planned. Eligible rewards are designed to be sent directly to holder wallets.

Why does the Reward Chest show ETH?

Creator-fee contributions arrive in ETH. A selected amount can later be converted into TSLA for holder rewards.

Are reward amounts fixed?

No. Reward amounts and distribution details may be determined separately for each reward cycle.

How is the creator fee allocated?

40% supports the Reward Chest, 35% supports children's charity and 25% supports continued project development.

Does the site update automatically?

The displayed Reward Chest ETH balance is read directly from the public Robinhood Chain wallet.
